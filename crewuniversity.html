What it is
Crew University uses aspects of the music business as a launchpad to teach real-world skills like leadership, financial literacy, and behind-the-scenes elements of the industry. But it's just as much about sisterhood, building confidence, and being courageous. It's built for girls who love music — but the skills and the growth apply to life as a student now, and to any career later.
